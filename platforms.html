AN INITIAL AREA OF FOCUS

Start with the work
that needs doing.

SITESERV is the first platform in the WORKBASE portfolio. It focuses on the temporary services and infrastructure that commercial construction projects rely on.

Construction projects rely on temporary power, water, connectivity, sanitation, fencing, lighting, security, equipment and other essential services. Coordinating those requirements means working across providers, schedules, deliveries, installations and ongoing changes. SITESERV is in development to bring these activities into a more structured process.

CONSTRUCTION-SITE SERVICES & INFRASTRUCTURE

SITESERV®

Site infrastructure and service coordination.

SITESERV is a construction operations platform in development. It will help project teams organize the temporary infrastructure and essential services a site depends on, bringing service requirements, provider coordination, procurement, scheduling and supporting records into one structured workflow.

The platform is designed around the full service lifecycle: identifying what a site needs, coordinating delivery and installation, managing ongoing service activity and changes, and organizing relocation or removal as project requirements evolve.

Rather than treating each service as a separate administrative task, SITESERV will give project teams a clearer way to organize requirements, responsibilities and the providers involved.
